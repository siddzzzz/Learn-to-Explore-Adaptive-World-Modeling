\subsection{Benchmark 1: Sample-Efficient Active Discovery}
In the 4-node Causal Lab benchmark, an agent must disambiguate candidate causal DAGs. Figure~1 in the supplementary documents reveals that HEx-WM reaches near-zero posterior entropy within 15 environment interactions, while Plan2Explore requires over 85 steps and ICM fails to converge within 100 steps.

\subsection{Benchmark 2: Robustness to Aleatoric Distraction (Noisy TV)}
Under an independent Gaussian noise channel ($\sigma = 2.0$), ICM and Plan2Explore experience irreversible policy collapse, allocating $>90\%$ of their action budget attempting to predict the unpredictable noise screen. In contrast, HEx-WM identifies the true governing rule in $12.4$ steps, confirming Theorem 1.

\subsection{Benchmark 3: Self-Driving Chemical Batch Reactor}
To evaluate real-world scientific impact, we deployed Safe-HEx-WM on a non-linear continuous chemical reactor governed by coupled Arrhenius ODEs. While standard Bayesian Optimization caused dangerous thermal spikes (exceeding 403.9 K and generating toxic cracking byproducts), Safe-HEx-WM systematically navigated the epistemic barrier envelope, achieving 99.5\% pure synthesis at 317.7 K with \textbf{zero safety violations}.
